1. Introduction

Welcome to Foco ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use the Foco mobile application (the "App"), which is a security alarm system controller for managing your Foco hardware devices.

2. Information We Collect

The Foco App operates primarily as a local device management tool. We collect the following types of information:

2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Device Information: Device names, SIM card numbers, device serial numbers, and device configuration settings for your Foco alarm system hardware.
  • User Profiles: User names, phone numbers, user numbers, partition numbers, and access mode settings for alarm system users.
  • Security Credentials: Passwords are stored locally using SHA-256 hashing with random salts. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Zone Configurations: Wireless zone names, numbers, types, and alert settings for your security sensors.
  • Voice Messages: Recorded voice alert messages (up to 15 seconds) stored locally on your device.
  • Auto Control Settings: Scheduled automation rules and relay configurations.
  • App Preferences: Theme settings, language preferences, and calendar type selections.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear: We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers. All data remains entirely on your local device. We have no access to your device information, alarm settings, user data, or any other content within the App.

3. How We Use Information

All information stored in the App is used solely for:

  • Managing and configuring your Foco alarm system hardware
  • Controlling security devices via SMS commands
  • Storing your security preferences and user configurations
  • Providing biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) for secure app access
  • Enabling backup and restore functionality for device data

4. Biometric Authentication

The App uses your device's biometric authentication system (Face ID on iOS, fingerprint on Android) to provide secure and convenient access. Biometric data is processed entirely on your device through the operating system's secure enclave. We do not have access to your biometric data, and it is never transmitted or stored by the App.

5. SMS Communication

The App communicates with your Foco alarm system hardware via SMS messages. These SMS messages are sent directly from your device to your alarm system's SIM card number. We do not intercept, read, or store any SMS content. SMS communication is a standard feature of your mobile carrier and is subject to your carrier's privacy policy.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
  • Facebook SDK or any social media tracking
  • Advertising networks or ad trackers
  • Crash reporting services that transmit personal data
  • Cloud storage services for user data

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are encrypted using SHA-256 with unique random salts
  • All data is stored locally on your device using secure storage
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • Biometric authentication is handled by your device's secure hardware

8. Data Backup and Export

The App allows you to create local backup files containing your device configurations. These backup files are stored on your device and can be exported via the system share sheet. You have full control over these files. We do not have access to your backup files.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
